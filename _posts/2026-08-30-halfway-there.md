myself that I wanted to complete a lot of them, but I never got around to doing that. However,
today, I finished the first 50 problems! It took me like two months to get to this point, but I'm really happy that I got this far. According to Project Euler's own data, the number of people who complete successive problems exponentially decreases. My goal is to get to 100 problems, so now I'm halfway there. I'm going to continue this momentum and keep moving forward.</p>
